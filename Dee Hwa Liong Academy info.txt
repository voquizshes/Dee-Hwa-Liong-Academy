MISSION AND VISION
MISSION 
DEE HWA LIONG ACADEMY aims to achieve its vision by developing young learners into intelligent thinking individuals and future responsible citizens who can cope with global advancements by:
• providing young children a venue to develop an integrated personality that is spiritually, morally and culturally enlightened; 
• preparing the learners for life skills in the increasingly scientific and technological world;
• committing itself to be a well-respected leading institution of learning with highly competent personnel who are committed to develop God-fearing, morally upright, self-reliant, socially responsible and well-disciplined individuals. notes
VISION 
Dee Hwa Liong Academy envisions itself as a center for academic excellence established for the purpose of molding the Filipino youth for the service of God, country and humanity.
