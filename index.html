OUR VISION

A center for academic excellence

Dee Hwa Liong Academy envisions itself as a center for academic excellence established for the purpose of molding the Filipino youth for the service of God, country and humanity.

OUR MISSION

Preparing learners for life

Dee Hwa Liong Academy aims to achieve its vision by developing young learners into intelligent thinking individuals and future responsible citizens who can cope with global advancements.

  • Provide a venue for young children to develop an integrated personality that is spiritually, morally, and culturally enlightened.
  • Prepare learners with life skills for an increasingly scientific and technological world.
  • Strive to be a respected leading institution with competent personnel, developing God-fearing, morally upright, self-reliant, socially responsible, and well-disciplined individuals.
